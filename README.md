# 🦏 My Bharatpur — Beyond the Safari Gate

A modern tourism website designed to help visitors discover **Bharatpur and the Chitwan region of Nepal** beyond the usual safari experience.

The website combines local attractions, an interactive map, a smart local guide named **Gaindā**, and a personalized day planner.

---

## 🌿 About the Project

**My Bharatpur — Beyond the Safari Gate** is a tourism-focused web project that highlights places, activities, nature, culture, and local experiences around Bharatpur and Chitwan.

The goal is to make exploring the region easier through a simple, interactive, and visually appealing website.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Map
- Interactive Leaflet map
- Shows important places around Bharatpur and Chitwan
- Includes locations such as:
  - Bharatpur
  - Sauraha
  - Chitwan National Park
  - Devghat
  - Meghauli
  - Beeshazari Tal
  - Kasara
  - Patihani
  - Bharatpur Hospital
  - Tourist Police Sauraha
  - Nepal Police Bharatpur

### 🦏 Gaindā — Local Guide
Gaindā is the website's local tourism assistant.

Users can ask questions about:
- Places to visit
- Places near the jungle
- Sauraha
- Travel cost
- Things to do in Chitwan

The guide also provides quick suggestion buttons for common questions.

### 🗓️ Build Your Chitwan Day
Visitors can select their interests and generate a simple day plan.

Available interests include:
- 🦏 Wildlife
- 🙏 Religious & culture
- 🌊 Lakes & nature
- 🥾 Adventure
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family
- 🍜 Food & local life

The planner creates suggestions for:
- 🌅 Morning
- ☀️ Afternoon
- 🌇 Evening

### 📱 Responsive Design
The website is designed to work across:
- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile devices
