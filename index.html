About Bharatpur

Bharatpur sits in the Chitwan valley of southern Nepal, where the Rapti and Narayani rivers cut through sal forest and open floodplain on their way down from the hills. It's best known as the gateway to Chitwan National Park, Nepal's first national park and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, home to one-horned rhinos, gharial crocodiles, and the occasional Bengal tiger. But the valley itself is just as much the draw: warm, subtropical, and green almost all year, dotted with villages, river confluences, hills, and parks that have their own slower rhythm. It's a place people pass through on the way to the jungle — worth stopping in for its own sake.
